What it does

VLMShift checks whether a vision-language model keeps its answers when one or more conditions change, alone or combined: the language of the question, the source of the image, its quality…

  • Compare one or several models across any condition in your table.
  • Measure whether a model does worse, or changes its mind on the same images.
  • Trust the numbers: each one comes with its uncertainty, and the report says what could not be measured.
  • Work safely: stop at any time, resume, and rerun the analysis without calling the models.

VLMShift is a research tool.
